Little Felt Garden Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Little Felt Garden is a picture puzzle app for young children, made by Frederico Menonca Passos de Lopes e Goncalves, trading as Orcas and Yetis. This policy explains what the Android app does with information. The app is made for children to use together with a grown-up, and it is built to need as little information as possible.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, no ads, no usage analytics and no tracking.
  • We do not ask for, and do not collect, your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or microphone.
  • Your child's progress, settings and PIN stay on your device.
  • The app uses the internet to download puzzles, to check that it is a genuine copy of the app, to check which packs you own with Google Play, and to send anonymous crash reports.
  • If a grown-up taps the bug button and sends a problem report, what they typed is sent to us, with a few technical details. Nothing is sent unless they tap Send.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

What stays on your device

The app saves the following on your device only. None of it is sent to us.

  • which puzzles are downloaded, and how far your child got in each puzzle, including which puzzles are finished;
  • settings, such as whether sound is muted and whether the grown-up lock uses press-and-hold or a PIN;
  • if you choose a PIN, a scrambled (salted and hashed) form of it, never the digits themselves, together with a count of wrong tries so the lock can slow down guessing;
  • which packs you own, as last reported by Google Play, so the app works offline;
  • which parts of the first-run tour have been shown.

All of this is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Downloading puzzles

Puzzles and the list of packs are downloaded from our server, which is hosted by Cloudflare. To protect the puzzles, the app first proves to our server that it is a genuine copy installed from Google Play on a genuine Android device. It does this with Google's Play Integrity service: the app asks Google for a token, and our server asks Google whether the token is valid. Google tells us only whether the check passed. It does not tell us who you are.

When the app starts a session, it sends our server:

  • a random installation ID, made by the app the first time it runs. It is not based on your device, your account or anything about you, and it is gone when you uninstall the app or clear its data;
  • the Play Integrity token described above;
  • the technical details every internet request carries, such as your IP address.

We use this only to deliver the puzzles and to keep them from being copied. We do not use it to identify you or your child, build profiles, or advertise. We do not keep our own logs of these requests. Our server host, Cloudflare, processes this technical data to deliver the service and keep it secure, under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

Some packs are sold as one-time in-app purchases through Google Play. Buying, payment and refunds happen entirely in Google Play, under Google's terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. The app asks Google Play which packs your Google account owns, so it can unlock them, and keeps that list on your device. Purchases are available only in the grown-up area of the app.

App updates

The app uses Google Play's in-app update feature to find out whether a newer version is available. This is handled by Google Play on your device.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, it can send a short technical report so we can find out what went wrong and fix it. We use these reports only to develop and improve the app. We do not use them for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell or share them.

A crash report contains technical details only:

  • what the app was doing when it crashed (a technical stack trace);
  • the app version, your device model, Android version and general device state, such as available memory;
  • an anonymous installation identifier made by the crash reporting service, which lets us count how many devices a problem affects. It is not linked to your name, email address or any account.

The reports are processed on our behalf by Google, using the Firebase Crashlytics service, and are kept there for a limited period. See Google's Firebase privacy information for how Google handles this data.

Problem reports

The app has a bug button. If you tap it, you can type a short title and description of a problem and tap Send. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send. We use reports only to understand and fix the problem.

A report contains:

  • the title and description you typed;
  • which screen you were on, and the pack or puzzle, if any, including the version of the puzzle files on your device;
  • the app version, your device manufacturer and model, and your Android version.

It does not include your name, email address, location, contacts or photos, unless you type them in. Please do not type personal information, and please do not let a child send a report. Reports are sent straight from the app to GitHub, which hosts our private issue tracker, and are stored there in a repository only we can read. GitHub receives the technical details every internet request carries, such as your IP address, under its own privacy policy. We keep a report for as long as it helps to fix the problem and then delete it. You can ask us to delete one sooner (see Contact).

Permissions

The app asks Android for one permission: access to the network, for the purposes above. It does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you or your child to create an account.
  • We do not use advertising or usage analytics services.
  • We do not link anything to your identity, and we do not sell or share information. A problem report goes only to our private issue tracker.
  • The app has no chat or social features, and no links to other websites except this policy and the terms.

Children

Little Felt Garden is made for young children to use together with a grown-up. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only data that leaves the device is the technical data described above: the random installation ID and integrity check used to download puzzles, the ownership check with Google Play, anonymous crash reports, and a problem report if a grown-up chooses to send one. Downloads, purchases and settings are available only in the grown-up area, which a child cannot open without pressing and holding or entering the PIN.

Your rights

Because we do not hold information that identifies you, there is generally nothing for us to look up or delete about you. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything stored on your device and the link between you and the random installation ID. If you believe we hold information about you, or you have any privacy question, contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding accounts or sharing, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change is released.
