Tiny Pieces Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Tiny Pieces is a photo puzzle for young children. A grown-up chooses or takes a photo, and the app turns it into a puzzle. This policy explains what the Android app and the web version do with that photo and with any other information. It applies to both, and it points out where they differ.

The short version

  • Photos stay on your device. They are never uploaded or shared.
  • The app has no accounts, no ads, no usage analytics and no tracking.
  • The Android app sends anonymous crash reports, only to help us find and fix bugs while we develop the game. Reports never include photos or personal information.
  • We do not collect personal information from you or your child.
  • The only thing the app remembers between visits is whether sound is muted.

Photos

You can take a photo with your camera or choose one from your photo library. The photo is resized and held in the memory of your device, only while you set up and play the puzzle. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

  • Android, choosing a photo: the app reads the photo you pick through the system photo picker. It does not copy or keep it.
  • Android, taking a photo: the system camera app saves the picture to a temporary file in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. The app loads it and then deletes the file straight away. It is not saved to your gallery. If the app is closed in the short moment between the camera saving the picture and the app deleting it, the temporary file can remain until Android clears the app's cache.
  • Web: the photo is opened by your browser and processed on your device. It disappears when you close the page or choose another photo.

When you choose another photo, or close the app, the photo is discarded. Tiny Pieces does not keep a library of your photos or your puzzles.

Camera access

The camera is used only when a grown-up taps the button to take a photo. On Android this opens your device's own camera app, so Tiny Pieces does not ask for camera permission itself. On the web, your browser may ask for permission before it opens the camera.

Crash reports (Android app only)

If the Android app crashes, it can send a short technical report so we can find out what went wrong and fix it. We use these reports strictly to help develop and improve the game. We do not use them for advertising, profiling or any other purpose, and we do not sell or share them.

A crash report contains technical details only:

  • what the app was doing when it crashed (a technical stack trace);
  • the app version, your device model, Android version and general device state, such as available memory and screen orientation;
  • an anonymous installation identifier, which lets us count how many devices a problem affects. It is not linked to your name, email address or any account.

Reports never include your photos, your puzzles, your name, your location or your contacts. The reports are processed on our behalf by Google, using the Firebase Crashlytics service, and are kept there for a limited period. See Google's Firebase privacy information for how Google handles this data.

This is why the Android app asks Android for permission to use the network. It uses the network only to send crash reports. Photos are never sent.

The web version does not send crash reports.

What the app stores

The app saves one setting on your device: whether sound is muted. It stays on your device and is not sent anywhere. Nothing else is saved.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, location or contacts.
  • We do not use advertising or usage analytics services.
  • We do not sell or share information. The only data that leaves your device is the anonymous crash report described above, and only from the Android app.
  • The Android app does not upload your photos, and it uses the internet only to send crash reports.

The web version and our host

The web version is a website. Like any website, the company that hosts it, Cloudflare, may process technical details of each request, such as your IP address, to deliver the page and keep it secure. We do not add analytics to the site and we do not use this information to identify you or your child.

Children

Tiny Pieces is made for young children to use together with a grown-up. The app does not collect personal information about a child. The only data that leaves the device is the anonymous, technical crash report described above, which is used only to fix bugs. Photos are chosen or taken by the grown-up and never leave the device.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by saving puzzles or adding sharing, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change is released.
